The product
Now that you understand the problem, the product will make sense. By the end of this section you can walk through what Journify does, how it is structured, and why each layer matters — in plain language.

Day 21 manager check-in — Product ready
You can walk through the five pillars and explain what each one does in plain language. Your manager asks follow-up questions. If it lands, you move to Phase 3 — your track. This is a conversation, not a review. If something is off, your manager will tell you and you'll work on it together.

1

Conversion events collected server-side before the browser loses them
2

3

Visibility
Unified view of what each ad platform is actually seeing and learning from
PillarWhat it doesWhy it matters
CaptureCollects conversion events server-side before browser restrictions can block themThis is what replaces the failing pixel — the foundation everything else depends on
MatchEnriches every event with available identifiers (email hash, phone hash, click IDs) to maximise the platform's ability to connect the event to a userHigher match rate = better AI learning = better campaign performance
ValidateChecks each event before sending — correct hashing, correct taxonomy, no duplicates, correct format for each platformMost vendors send what they receive. Journify sends what the platform needs.
DeliverSends validated events via official Conversion APIs in real time to Meta, TikTok, Snap, Google, and AmazonPlatform-native delivery means the signal is trusted and acted on correctly
VisibilityA unified dashboard showing what each platform is actually seeing — match rates, event volume, anomalies, health scoresWithout this, you're flying blind. Visibility turns signal quality into a managed metric.
Read the Product Overview
~30 min
The Product Overview covers the five pillars in depth — Capture, Match, Validate, Deliver, Visibility. Focus on what each pillar does and why the sequence matters. Each stage affects the next. Understanding the dependency chain is what lets you explain the product logically rather than as a list of features.
Accuracy first. Confidence follows. Don't try to memorise everything — understand the logic of why the sequence is the way it is. That understanding is what makes you credible.
Read the product roadmap overview
~15 min
A clear picture of what is live today and what is coming next. This is important for two reasons — you need to know what you can and can't promise customers, and you need to understand the direction the product is heading so you can position it confidently in forward-looking conversations. Attribution is on the roadmap — that context matters.
Day 21 manager check-in — product ready check-in
~20 min · Conversational
Walk your manager through the five pillars as if explaining the product to someone outside the industry. Accuracy matters more than fluency at this stage. Your manager will ask follow-up questions — what does Validate mean in practice, why does the sequence matter, what's the difference between Journify and just using the pixel. This is the last check-in before Phase 3.
The bar is plain language, not technical depth. If you can explain each pillar in one clear sentence and connect it to the customer's problem, you're ready. Technical depth comes with Phase 3.
