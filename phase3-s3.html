Phase 3 · Section 3 · Day 29–30
Shadowing & role-play
You know the framework. Now you watch it live, reflect on it, and practise it. By Day 30 you can run a discovery call independently. Your manager certifies you — and Phase 3 is complete.
Day 29–30 AE track 4 tasks
Day 30 manager check-in — Discovery ready
You run a mock discovery call with your manager playing a prospect. They assess whether you can open well, ask the right questions, apply BANT naturally, and close with a clear next step. If it lands — you're discovery ready and Phase 3 is complete.
Step 1 — Shadow 2–3 discovery calls
What to watch for — not just what they say
Ask your manager to add you to 2–3 live discovery calls before Day 30
Shadowing is only useful if you're watching with intent. Don't just listen — observe the structure, the silences, and the moments where the conversation shifts.
The open
How is rapport built without wasting time? When does the agenda get set?
The listening
How long is the silence after a question? How is the prospect guided without being led?
The pain moment
When does the prospect reveal real pain? How is that moment caught and deepened?
Objections
What objections come up? How are they acknowledged first, then addressed?
The close
How does the call end? Is there always a clear next step agreed before hanging up?
After each shadow session — write your debrief
What was the prospect's main pain — and at what point in the call did it surface?
What question or phrase opened the prospect up the most?
What would you do differently if you were running that call?
Send the debrief to your manager after each call — it signals that you're building a mental model of how good discovery works.
Step 2 — Elevator pitch
Deliver your elevator pitch — under 60 seconds
Practise until it flows without notes — you'll use this to open every call
The elevator pitch is not a product demo. It's a door-opener — a clear, confident statement of what Journify does and why the person on the other end should care. Under 60 seconds. Leave the prospect curious, not informed.
Journify elevator pitch — learn this
"Ad platforms like Meta and TikTok have become AI-driven systems — they optimise based on the conversion signals brands send them. The problem is that most brands are sending 30 to 40% fewer signals than they actually have, because of iOS restrictions and browser tracking limitations. That means the AI is learning from incomplete data — which is why ROAS becomes volatile and CAC keeps rising. Journify fixes that. We capture, validate, and deliver clean conversion signals directly to the platforms — so the AI works from complete data, and performance reflects what's actually happening in the business."
Say it out loud. Alone. Then to your manager. Then to someone outside Journify who knows nothing about AdTech. If they understand it — you've got it.
Step 3 — Role-play scenarios
3 role-play scenarios with your manager
Do these before your Day 30 check-in — they are your preparation for it
Scenario 1
Cold open — prospect doesn't know signal loss
Manager plays a Head of Performance Marketing who has never heard of signal loss. Open well, ask the right questions, surface the pain, explain Journify without jargon.
Goal: surface the pain naturally
Scenario 2
Objection — "we already have an MMP"
Manager plays a prospect who says "we already use AppsFlyer — why do we need Journify?" Acknowledge, reframe, and explain the difference without dismissing their current setup.
Goal: handle without dismissing
Scenario 3
Full mock discovery call — end to end
Manager plays a qualified prospect (eCommerce brand, $60K/month ad spend, running Meta and Snap). Run the full call — open, questions, pain identification, BANT, and close with a clear next step.
Goal: run the full structure
Day 30 — manager check-in
What your manager will assess
Your manager runs Scenario 3 again — but this time it counts. They play a prospect and you run the full discovery call. Afterwards they ask you to self-assess, then give feedback on four areas:
Did you open with rapport and set a clear agenda?
Did you ask open questions and listen — or lead the prospect to answers?
Did BANT feel natural in the conversation — or like a checklist being read?
Did the call end with a clear, agreed next step?
Discovery readiness is a standard — not a date. Most people get there by Day 30. Some need a few more days. Both are fine — your manager will tell you if something needs more work.
