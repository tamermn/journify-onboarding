Watch first — from the founders
What Journify is
In one sentence
"Journify manages and improves the conversion signals sent to ad platforms so AI-driven optimization reflects real business outcomes."
The problem we solve
Why Journify exists
Ad platforms have become AI-driven learning systems — they optimize on the signals brands send them. When those signals are incomplete or unreliable due to iOS restrictions, cookie deprecation, or missing server-side tracking, ad algorithms make bad decisions. Budgets get wasted. ROAS becomes volatile. Teams lose trust in their data. This is a data problem, not a media problem — and it's the problem Journify fixes.
The product — three layers
Layer A
Connections
Infrastructure — server-side delivery and Conversion API connections to ad platforms
Layer B
Data health
Reliability — validates, aligns, and monitors signal quality continuously
Layer C
Signals
Visibility — cross-platform performance dashboard connected to Meta, TikTok, Google, Snap
Coming soon
Attribution
Connecting spend to real business outcomes — built on clean signal infrastructure
Where we operate
Markets and customers
Journify operates across the GCC and internationally, with primary markets in UAE and Saudi Arabia. Our customers are performance marketing leaders, growth teams, and agencies managing meaningful ad spend who need reliable signal infrastructure they can trust and control.
