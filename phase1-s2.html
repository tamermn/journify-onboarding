The fastest way to stop feeling lost in conversations. Every term you'll hear in your first weeks — in plain language, with context for why it matters to a customer. Come back to this page whenever a term comes up in a call that you're unsure about.
The problem
Signal loss Problem
When a purchase happens but the ad platform never finds out. Affecting most brands at 30–40% of purchases — not a rare edge case.
Customer pain: volatile ROAS they can't explain
Browser pixel Problem
The old way of tracking conversions. A snippet that fires on purchase or sign-up. iOS 14, ad blockers, and browser restrictions mean it now misses a significant share of events.
Most brands still rely on it — they think they have tracking, they don't have complete tracking
iOS ATT Problem
Apple's 2021 change requiring tracking permission. ~70% of users said no. This is the structural event that made Journify necessary.
The "why now" behind the entire market existing
Match rate Problem
% of conversions the platform can match to a real user profile. The single most important number in signal quality.
40% match rate = the AI is working from 40% of data
The product
CAPI Product
Conversion API. Sends conversion data server-to-server, bypassing the browser entirely. The fix for signal loss.
CAPI is how conversions reach Meta when the pixel can't
Server-side tracking Product
Any tracking that happens on the server rather than the browser. More reliable, less exposed to privacy restrictions. Journify is built on this infrastructure.
Having it isn't the same as having healthy signals — ask about match rate
Event validation Product
Journify checks every event before sending — correct hashing, no duplicates, correct taxonomy per platform. Most vendors don't do this.
Most vendors transmit. Journify validates first.
Deduplication Product
Removes duplicate conversion events when pixel and CAPI both fire for the same event. Prevents inflated numbers and confused algorithms.
Brands with both running undeduped see inflated numbers unknowingly
Data health Product
Journify's ongoing monitoring layer — match rates, event consistency, anomaly detection, alerts when quality degrades. Continuous, not one-time.
An agency set it up once. Journify keeps it working every day.
Performance metrics
ROAS Metric
Return on Ad Spend. Revenue generated for every dollar spent on ads. If ROAS = 4x, a $1,000 campaign returned $4,000 in revenue.
The primary KPI in almost every conversation — signal loss makes it volatile
CAC Metric
Customer Acquisition Cost. Total spend divided by customers acquired. Signal loss inflates reported CAC because missed conversions make spend look less efficient than it is.
Poor signal quality means customers are likely underreporting actual acquisitions
LTV Metric
Lifetime Value. Total revenue a customer generates over their relationship with a brand. Pairs with CAC to define how much a brand can afford to spend to acquire a customer.
LTV:CAC ratio is a key health indicator — Journify helps make the CAC side more accurate
CPM Metric
Cost Per Mille — cost per 1,000 impressions. The standard bidding unit on Meta, TikTok, Snap, and Google Display.
Rising CPMs with no targeting change often signals degraded data quality
CPL Metric
Cost Per Lead. Cost to generate one qualified lead. Common in B2B and lead-gen campaigns — particularly relevant in the GCC market.
Signal loss inflates CPL — missed lead events make campaigns look less effective
CPC Metric
Cost Per Click. What a brand pays each time someone clicks their ad. Less central than ROAS in most conversations but useful efficiency context.
Useful surface-level health indicator when customers talk about efficiency without specifics
AdTech ecosystem
MMP Ecosystem
Mobile Measurement Partner. Tools like AppsFlyer, Adjust, and Branch that track app installs and in-app events. Common in the GCC. MMPs don't replace CAPI for web conversion signals.
Many customers think their MMP covers signal quality — it doesn't replace server-side web tracking
CDP Ecosystem
Customer Data Platform. Tools like Segment that unify customer data into one profile. CDPs store data — Journify activates it by sending clean signals to ad platforms.
Customers often confuse CDPs with what Journify does — knowing the difference sharpens positioning
First-party data Ecosystem
Data a brand owns directly — purchase history, emails, CRM records. Ad platforms use it for targeting and matching. First-party data is the input; Journify is the infrastructure that delivers it correctly.
Customers mention it as the "solution" — Journify is how they actually activate it
GTM Ecosystem
Google Tag Manager. How most brands currently manage tracking pixels — a browser-side tag container. Browser-side only, which is exactly why signal quality suffers.
"We use GTM for tracking" is an entry point — GTM alone misses a significant share of conversions
The three sentences every Journify person says the same way
"Ad platforms allocate budgets based on the conversions they receive. Most brands are sending 30 to 40% fewer than they actually have. We fix that."
