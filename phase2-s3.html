Day 13–14 Day 14 manager check-in 3 tasks
Day 14 manager check-in — Industry ready
Your manager sits with you for 20 minutes. They ask you to explain signal loss as if talking to a performance marketer who has never heard the term. Two or three follow-up questions. If it lands, you move to the product. If something is still fuzzy, you spend another day or two on it together. That is not a setback — that is how learning works at Journify.
Read the ICP documentation
~20 min
Understand exactly who Journify targets — the company profile, the persona, the trigger conditions, and the disqualifiers. Knowing the ICP precisely is what separates reps who book the right meetings from those who waste time on poor fits.
Geography
UAE and Saudi Arabia primary · GCC broader · International selectively
Customer type
Performance marketing leaders, growth teams, and agencies managing meaningful ad spend
Trigger signals
Running paid media on Meta, TikTok, Snap, or Google · Concerned about ROAS volatility or attribution gaps
Disqualifiers
No active paid media spend · Brand-only budgets · No technical or marketing ops capability
ICP documentation — Notion
Review 2–3 case studies
~20–30 min
Real results from real customers in your market. Read 2–3 case studies from the Journify website — focus on the problem framing, the metric improvement, and how the customer's situation maps to what you've learned about signal loss. These become your proof points in discovery calls.
When a prospect says "prove it works," your answer lives here. Know at least one case study well enough to reference naturally in a conversation without sounding like you're reading from a sheet.
Customer stories — journify.io/customers
Day 14 manager check-in — industry ready
~20 min · Verbal, conversational
Sit down with your manager. They ask you to explain signal loss to a performance marketer who's never heard the term. Then two or three follow-up questions — match rate, what iOS ATT changed, why the pixel isn't enough. This is a conversation, not a test. If something is still fuzzy, your manager will tell you and you'll work on it together.
You're ready when you can explain the problem in terms of what a customer has already experienced — not in terms of technology. "Your ROAS is volatile and you can't explain why" is the right framing. "iOS 14 deprecated IDFA tracking" is not.
