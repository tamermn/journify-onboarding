Digital marketing foundations
Before you can understand the customer's pain, you need to understand how they work. These platform certifications build the foundation — how ad platforms make decisions, what campaigns look like from the inside, and the language your customers use every day.
Day 8–10 4 core platforms Free certifications
How to approach these
These are official platform certifications — free and self-paced. Answers can be found online, but go through them properly. The goal is to think the way your customers think when they're setting up campaigns — not to pass a test. That perspective is what makes you credible in discovery calls.
Google Core
Google Skillshop — first 3 courses recommended · ~3–4 hours
Google's Skillshop covers the fundamentals of digital marketing and Google Ads. Focus on the first 3 courses in the Fundamentals of Digital Marketing path — they give you the strongest baseline for understanding how search and display campaigns work, how budgets are set, and how conversion tracking fits in.

Meta Core
Meta Blueprint — 6 courses · ~3–4 hours
Meta Blueprint covers campaign objectives, ad buying, audience targeting, and campaign management. These are the courses that map most directly to what Journify's customers talk about — ROAS, conversion events, pixel setup, and optimisation. This is the platform where signal loss has the most visible impact.

Snapchat Core — GCC priority
Snapchat Focus — 2 collections · ~2–3 hours
Snapchat represents a major share of Journify clients' spend due to its popularity across the GCC market. This makes it unusually important compared to most other markets. Understanding how Snapchat campaigns are structured and activated helps you speak the same language as clients who are spending significant budget here.
GCC context: Snapchat has outsized importance in Saudi Arabia and UAE compared to global norms. Your clients will reference it constantly — don't treat it as secondary.

TikTok Core
TikTok Academy — Media Buying Expert path · ~2–3 hours
TikTok's learning path covers campaign setup, ad formats, performance marketing, and measurement on the platform. TikTok has a distinct campaign structure compared to Meta and Google — understanding it rounds out your platform knowledge and matters as TikTok spend grows across the region.
