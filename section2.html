Section 2 — Tools & operational setup
A quick orientation to the tools that make up Journify's operating environment. You'll know most of these — the goal is simply to confirm everything is set up and understand how each one fits into the workflow here.

What's expected here
Setup and orientation, not training. Core tools need to be live and accessible before Day 1. Awareness tools — the more specialised revenue stack — will be covered in depth during onboarding. For now, understand their role and where they fit.

Active from Day 1
Standard hardware across the company. If you've been on Windows recently, a quick refresh on macOS navigation and shortcuts will save time on Day 1 — particularly Finder, Spotlight search, and window management.

Google Workspace
Active from Day 1
The full collaboration layer at Journify — email, calendar, meetings, and shared docs. Internal collaboration runs primarily through Google Meet; Zoom is used for external and customer-facing calls.

Slack
Active from Day 1
Primary async communication across the company. Most decisions, updates, and cross-team coordination happen in channels — keep your notifications configured so you're in the loop without being overwhelmed.
