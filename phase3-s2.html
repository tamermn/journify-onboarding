The discovery mindset
Most reps show up to a discovery call ready to talk about their product. The best ones show up ready to listen. Your job in this section is to internalise the framework — so that on a real call you're not reading from a list. You're having a conversation with a structure in your head.
Step 1 — Qualify before you call
Applying the ICP — is this account worth a call?

Decision rule — when not to advance
If a prospect fails 3 or more ICP criteria → do not advance to a discovery call. Move them to a long-term nurture sequence. A discovery call with a poor-fit prospect wastes your time, their time, and inflates your pipeline with deals that will never close.
Step 2 — Pre-call research
5 things to know before every discovery call
Do this the night before or morning of — never wing it
Review their website and LinkedIn — understand what they sell and how they go to market
Confirm they are running paid ads on Meta, TikTok, Google, or Snapchat
Check their eCommerce setup — Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, or custom
Look for recent funding announcements, growth signals, or expansion news
Identify their current marketing tools — CDPs, attribution tools, analytics platforms
Have one good observation ready that shows you did your homework. "I noticed you're running campaigns on Snapchat — that's a significant investment for most GCC brands" opens more doors than a cold intro.
Step 3 — The discovery call
4-part discovery call structure
Use this as a mental map — not a script to read from
1
Open — rapport and agenda
Build rapport briefly — reference something from your research
Confirm the agenda and time available
"Before we dive in, could you share how your team currently manages paid media performance?"
2
Understand their business and ad strategy
What industry and business model? (eCommerce, DTC, marketplace, multi-location)
What is their monthly ad spend?
Which ad platforms? (Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat)
How do they track performance today? (GA, manual, CDP, attribution tool)
How do they handle first-party data activation?
3
Pain points and impact
What metrics do they track? (ROAS, CAC, LTV)
How do they segment audiences for retargeting and lookalike campaigns?
What happens when they lose attribution data?
Have they tried other solutions? What worked or didn't?
4
BANT qualification
Budget — what is their budget for improving attribution and ad performance?
Authority — who are the decision-makers for new martech tools?
Need — how important is solving this in the next 1–3 months?
Timeline — actively looking now, or a future priority?
Ask a question, then be quiet. Let the prospect tell you where the pain is — don't lead them to it.

Step 5 — After the call
What to do within 24 hours of every discovery call
This is where deals are won or lost — in the follow-through
Log everything in Attio — call notes, BANT outcome, lead score, next activity
Send a personalised follow-up email recapping key challenges discussed
If score 20+ — schedule demo call within 3–5 business days
If not an immediate fit — move to nurture sequence with educational content and case studies
Move deal to correct Attio stage — never leave it at Outreach after a call has happened
