The Journify sales funnel — 7 stages
Tap any stage to see key activities and exit criteria
1 Prospecting SDR 0%
Key activities: Assign accounts to SDRs by territory or vertical. Research to confirm ICP fit — industry, size, GCC geography. Identify decision-makers: Heads of Marketing, Growth, Founders. Enrich contacts via LinkedIn, Clay, or Keyplay.

Exit criteria: Accounts enriched and decision-makers identified.
2 Outreach SDR 15%
Key activities: Contact leads via email, LinkedIn, phone, and WhatsApp. Value-driven messaging anchored on the prospect's specific pain. Nurture sequences using case studies and content.

Exit criteria: Discovery call booked with a relevant decision-maker.
3 Qualification AE 30%
Key activities: Run discovery call, confirm pain points, apply BANT, verify ICP fit. Ask about ad spend, platforms, current tracking, attribution pain. Present Journify framed around match rate and ROAS improvement.

Exit criteria: Qualified opportunity — mutual interest confirmed, pain validated, next step agreed.
4 Demo AE 50%
Key activities: Deliver a tailored demo scoped to their platforms and use case. Address early objections, align on value, confirm budget range and key stakeholders.

Exit criteria: Prospect agrees to proceed to proposal.

Leadership may join for senior stakeholder conversations — but you own demo preparation and delivery.
5 Proposal AE 65%
Key activities: Send proposal with pricing and success plan via Qwilr. Confirm receipt and book follow-up within 48 hours. Track engagement on the proposal link.

Exit criteria: Prospect acknowledges receipt of proposal.

Leadership reviews and approves commercial terms before sending — coordinate with them on pricing.
6 Negotiation AE 80%
Key activities: Handle objections on pricing, competition, and timing. Finalise terms and navigate legal and procurement. You lead this stage. Leadership supports on commercial terms, pricing approvals, and senior stakeholder conversations as needed.

Exit criteria: Verbal or written commitment received.
7 Closed AE + SM Won / Lost
Won: Contract signed. Hand off to Success Manager. Document deal size, time to close, and key learnings in Attio. Pipeline review with CRO within 48 hours.

Lost: Log the reason — budget, no pain, no response 60+ days, competitor (who + why), champion left, project on hold. Every closed lost is a learning — document it properly.
Stage probabilities drive pipeline coverage calculations in Attio — they inform how the team weighs your pipeline against quota.
Your role in the funnel
SDR
Prospecting → Outreach → Booked meeting. Hands to AE at Qualification.
AE — you
Qualification → Demo → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed. You own every stage from first meeting to close.
Leadership
Supports on commercial terms, pricing approvals, and senior stakeholder conversations. Does not own any stage — you do.
Success Manager
Post Closed Won. Onboarding, implementation, and relationship management.
BANT qualification framework
B
Budget
Monthly ad spend $40K+? Do they have budget for a new martech investment?
A
Authority
Are we speaking with a decision-maker? Who approves new tools — CMO, Head of Growth, Founder?
N
Need
ROAS volatility? Attribution gaps? Rising CAC? Do they feel the signal loss pain?
T
Timeline
Actively looking now? Urgency to implement in the next 30–90 days?

Pipeline hygiene rules — non-negotiable
Log within 24h
Every call, email, and meeting logged in Attio within 24 hours. No exceptions.
Always a next step
Every open deal must have a next activity scheduled. No hanging deals.
60-day rule
Prospect silent 60+ days = Closed Lost. Log the reason. No zombie pipeline.
