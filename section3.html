Section 3 — Intro to digital marketing & AdTech
Journify operates at the intersection of signal loss and attribution. This section gives you the industry context to understand the problem space, customer pain, and where Journify sits before Week 2 begins.

What's expected here
Exposure and context, not expertise. The goal is to have heard these concepts before Week 2 begins — so the industry language and customer pain points feel familiar when they come up in meetings, not foreign. Deep dives happen during onboarding.

The 6 topics
Topic 1
What is digital advertising?
How brands pay to reach people online — across search, social, apps, and display. The commercial foundation that everything else in this section builds on.
Journify's customers are the brands running these campaigns. Understanding their world is the starting point for every commercial conversation.
The digital advertising ecosystem, explained (2024) ~12 min
What is digital advertising? A beginner's explanation ~8 min
Topic 2
Why brands advertise online
The business goals behind digital spend — awareness, consideration, and conversion. Understanding what drives budget decisions is what separates a transactional rep from a consultative one.
When a prospect talks about "driving conversions" or "improving ROAS," this is the context. Knowing their goals shapes how you frame Journify's value from the first call.
What is a marketing funnel? Explained ~10 min
Topic 3
Customer acquisition basics
How businesses measure the efficiency of acquiring new customers — CAC, LTV, and ROAS. These metrics dominate commercial conversations at the level you'll be operating.
CAC, LTV, and ROAS will come up in almost every discovery call. Being fluent in these from Day 1 signals credibility before you've even pitched the product.
How to calculate LTV and CAC ~12 min
Topic 4
Intro to tracking
The technical layer that measurement depends on — pixels, cookies, events, and tags. Understanding how tracking works conceptually helps you diagnose customer pain and ask sharper discovery questions.
When customers talk about "broken tracking" or "missing conversion data," this is the technical root cause. You don't need to be an engineer — but knowing the language earns trust early.
From cookies to pixels: website tracking explained (2024) ~14 min
Topic 5
Intro to signal loss
Why tracking data is becoming structurally less reliable — iOS privacy changes, cookie deprecation, ad blockers, and evolving regulation. Signal loss is the industry-wide problem Journify was built to solve.
This is the "why now" and the "why Journify" in one. Signal loss is the primary customer problem — understanding it deeply is the foundation of every pitch you'll make.
What is signal loss? ~15 min
Topic 6
Intro to attribution
How brands connect ad spend to outcomes — which channels, campaigns, and touchpoints actually drove a conversion. Attribution is the visibility signal loss erodes and the capability Journify restores.
Attribution is Journify's core direction and product answer. Signal loss creates the problem; attribution is what we restore. Understanding this distinction is what makes your pitch credible.
Measurement vs. attribution in digital marketing ~12 min
